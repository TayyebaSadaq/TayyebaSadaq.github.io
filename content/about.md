# About Me
Hi, I'm Tayyeba (also known as @codedbytea). I'm a UK-based reporting analyst transitioning into cybersecurity — specifically threat intelligence, security operations, and digital forensics. I graduated with a First Class Honours in Computer Science from the University of Derby, and I'm currently pursuing a part-time MSc in Cyber Security at the Open University.

## Who I am
My background sits at an interesting intersection: a BSc grounded in software engineering, an MSc focused on cyber security, and day-to-day work that's all about data integrity and audit trails. I understand how software is built, where things go wrong, and how to trace what happened when they do. I care about evidence, traceability, and making sure conclusions are explainable — whether that's a reporting pipeline or an incident investigation. I'm drawn to work where the details matter, where being methodical isn't optional, and where getting it right actually counts.

## What I'm building toward
I'm working toward roles in threat intelligence, SOC analysis, or digital forensics — the investigative, pattern-finding side of security. Everything I've done so far, from automating audit-ready reports to building explainable AI tools, has been sharpening the same underlying skill: the ability to trace, understand, and clearly communicate what happened and why. I'm building hands-on experience where I can, alongside my MSc.
